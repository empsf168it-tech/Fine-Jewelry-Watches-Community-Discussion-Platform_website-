Society Ethos

Our Four Guiding Commitments

Scholarly Provenance

Insisting on factual verification, historical archival evidence, and mechanical authenticity.

Gemological Purity

Promoting transparent disclosure of all gemstone treatments, enhancements, and certifications.

Independent Craft

Championing independent ateliers and bespoke artisans keeping artisanal watchmaking alive.

Collector Education

Empowering new collectors through buying advice, care guides, and open mentorship.
